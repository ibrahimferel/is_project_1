DES step by step

{% include "icons/warning.svg" %}Educational only. DES's 56-bit key can be brute-forced; the app stores a DES copy only because the assignment requires it, and reads data from the AES copy.

DES is a Feistel network: each round computes Li = Ri-1 and Ri = Li-1 XOR f(Ri-1, Ki). Decryption is the same procedure with the round keys reversed. The default key and block give the textbook result 85e813540f0ab405.
