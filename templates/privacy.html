What data is protected, and why

This app stores data that counts as personal data under the EU's GDPR and Indonesia's UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (UU PDP). Both laws require the controller to keep it secure (GDPR Art. 32, UU PDP Pasal 35). Encryption also matters after a breach: under GDPR Art. 34(3)(a), data that was encrypted and unintelligible to the attacker need not be individually notified.
